The distinction

Relevance is not enough.

An ad can match a topic and still waste attention. Helpfulness adds a stricter question: did the ad make the person’s next decision easier, clearer, safer, faster, or more informed?

Relevant Advertising

“This might interest you.”

Relevance is about correspondence: the right subject, audience, context, or intent.

context + audience + message → relevance
Helpful Advertising

“This helps you decide or act.”

Helpfulness is about utility: an ad contributes information, guidance, comparison, resolution, or a useful next step.

relevance + moment + utility + respect → helpfulness
The Helpfulness Lens

Five questions before an ad deserves attention.

This is a conceptual lens, not a claimed industry standard. It gives HelpfulAds.com a usable language for evaluating the experience an ad creates.

01 / NEED

Need

Does the ad address a real task, question, problem, or decision?

If no need exists, attention is being borrowed.
02 / MOMENT

Moment

Is it useful now — in this context — rather than merely plausible in general?

Timing converts relevance into usefulness.
03 / CLARITY

Clarity

Does it make the offer, trade-off, price, or reason to act easier to understand?

Confusion is not persuasion.
04 / UTILITY

Utility

Does the ad add guidance, comparison, proof, a tool, or a next useful action?

The ad should contribute, not merely appear.
05 / RESPECT

Respect

Is the experience transparent, proportionate, controllable, and privacy-aware?

Help without respect becomes pressure.
